SlayerBase Site Plan

Overview

Purpose

To provide cards and relics so that players can easily reference their abilities and effects.

Audience

Those who are playing Slay the Spire and want to see what every card/relic does.

Dynamic elements

Based on the search the user provides, the site will dynamically filter and display relevant cards and relics.
